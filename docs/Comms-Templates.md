# Communications templates

Generic, English. Replace `<Company>`, `<date>`, `<time>`, `<help desk>` and the placeholders.

## T-14 — announcement

Subject: Your workplace is moving to a new Microsoft 365 environment on `<date>`

On `<date>`, `<Company>` moves its email, files and Teams to a new Microsoft 365 environment.
You will receive a **new PC** that signs you in to the new environment.

What you need to know now:

- Keep working as normal until the cutover weekend — nothing changes before `<date>`.
- Everything you keep in OneDrive, your mailbox and your calendar moves automatically.
- Files stored **outside** OneDrive (local folders, PST files) do not move. Check your PC and
  tell `<help desk>` about anything stored locally before `<date - 7 days>`.

More instructions follow closer to the date.

---

## T-7 — preparation

Subject: One week to go — two small things to do

1. When your new PC arrives you may sign in to it once with your new account to register MFA —
   but **do not open Outlook, OneDrive or Teams on it yet**. Your data arrives at the cutover.
2. Check your old PC for files stored outside OneDrive and flag them to `<help desk>`.

Cutover starts `<date> <time>`; the old environment goes quiet then.

## T-1 — tomorrow it happens

Subject: Tomorrow `<date>`: the move happens

- Keep using the old PC as normal today.
- Leave it **switched on** over the weekend — it's our reference if anything is missing.
- On `<date + 1>` (Monday), start work on the **new PC** with your new account.
- Expect to approve MFA once and to wait while Outlook builds a new profile.

---

## Cutover day — it's live

Subject: The new environment is live — start on your new PC

The move is done. Sign in on your **new PC** with your new account. Outlook sets itself up;
mail, files, calendar and Teams are there. If anything looks wrong, contact `<help desk>` —
your old PC is still intact as a fallback reference.

---

## T+1 — first working day

Subject: Working in the new environment — known issues and fixes

Your day-1 checklist is in **After-Cutover-User-Guide** (attached / on <intranet>) — follow it
for the new Outlook profile, meeting re-acceptance and mobile setup.

- Meetings with **external guests may need a fresh invitation** — re-send if guests can't join.
- A meeting series where **one occurrence was edited** keeps an old link — re-send the series.
- Mobile mail/Teams: remove the old work account, add the new one.
- Shared mailboxes and delegated permissions were restored — report any you expected that are
  missing.

---

## T+7 — settling in

Subject: One week in — last checks

Everything should be fully working by now. Please report anything still missing or odd to
`<help desk>` — the old environment's remaining items are cleaned up soon, and this is the
moment to catch anything left behind.
